Less presentation. More participation.

Turn cybersecurity conversations into shared experiences

Security training often becomes another presentation, another checklist or another hour of people pretending to pay attention.

CyberSec Games takes a different approach.

Using carefully designed physical and collaborative games, teams explore cybersecurity challenges together, discuss competing priorities and practise making decisions in a safe environment.

As a CyberSec Games Facilitator, Cássio guides the experience, keeps participants engaged and connects the lessons from the game to the organisation’s real-world context.

A deliberate learning journey

How a facilitated session works

  1. 01

    Understand the objective

    Before the session, we discuss the audience, business context, current challenges and expected outcomes.

  2. 02

    Select the right game

    The experience is matched to the team, topic, technical depth and available time.

  3. 03

    Facilitate the session

    The facilitator introduces the game, explains the rules, encourages participation and keeps discussions productive.

  4. 04

    Debrief the experience

    The session closes with a structured conversation about decisions, patterns, risks and lessons.

  5. 05

    Apply the learning

    Insights can be converted into recommendations, actions, requirements or next steps.

FAQ

Good questions before the game begins

Do participants need cybersecurity experience?

No. The game and facilitation are adapted to the audience.

Can the session use our own architecture or scenario?

Yes. Depending on the objective, the session can use a fictional exercise, adapted scenario or real system context.

Can sessions be delivered remotely?

Yes. Remote sessions can combine physical cards, digital collaboration tools and supported remote-play formats.

How long does a session take?

Most sessions range from approximately 90 minutes to a full day.

How many people can participate?

The number depends on the game and format. Sessions can support a focused team, multiple tables or recurring conference groups.

Can the session be part of a company event?

Yes. Sessions can be delivered as workshops, conference activities, cybersecurity villages, internal training or team-building experiences.

Is the session available in Portuguese and English?

Yes. Sessions can be facilitated in English or Brazilian Portuguese.
