Security is a team activity

Security should not be a spectator sport.

The CyberSec Games Village turns part of a conference floor into an active, social learning environment. Attendees do not simply hear about cybersecurity. They make decisions, debate trade-offs, test assumptions and experience the consequences.

The format works around the conference rather than fighting it. Visitors can join a ten-minute challenge, book a facilitated game, compete with a team or return throughout the day to complete their Cyber Passport.

Why it works

Serious learning without the serious faces.

The Village creates a low-pressure environment where people can practise security thinking before the stakes become real.

1

Accessible participation

No prior game or cybersecurity experience is required for the entry-level activities.

2

Cross-functional collaboration

Games give technical and non-technical participants a shared language and common problem.

3

Memorable decisions

Players remember the trade-off they made, the attack they missed and the teammate who saw it first.

4

Conference energy

Visible play, leaderboards and finals create natural movement and repeat visits throughout the day.
