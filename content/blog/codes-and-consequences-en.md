# AppSec is learned by making decisions

Traditional training can explain concepts. The problem is that real work rarely presents a question with four options and only one correct answer.

In practice, teams must balance delivery, security, budget, deadlines, reputation and risk. Every choice solves one problem and may create another. **Codes & Consequences** turns that tension into a learning experience.

It is a strategic Application Security training game that simulates development cycles inspired by agile methods. Participants make decisions as a technology team and must deliver a functional system without losing control of security.

## How the experience works

During each cycle, the team decides where to invest its resources. New features compete with fixes, controls and security activities. Vulnerabilities, incidents, unexpected events and cyber threats change the situation throughout the journey.

The game does not reward the team that simply chooses security in every round. It requires balance.

Participants need to consider:

- business value and functionality;
- technical risk and vulnerabilities;
- budget and team capacity;
- product reputation;
- consequences created by previous decisions.

The objective is to reach the end with a product that works, preserves resources and maintains an acceptable level of risk.

## Why use a game

A decision that people experience is easier to remember than a rule shown on a slide. When a harmless-looking choice creates an incident two rounds later, the relationship between cause and consequence stops being abstract.

The activity helps teams discuss topics such as:

- security debt;
- vulnerability prioritization;
- trade-offs between speed and protection;
- communication between development, product and security;
- the accumulated impact of small decisions;
- shared responsibility across the SDLC.

Learning happens in the conversation between participants. No tool makes the decision on behalf of the team.

## Who it was created for

Codes & Consequences works as an activity for developers, AppSec professionals, DevOps, Security Champions, Product Owners, technical leaders and other people involved in software development.

It can also be used in:

- corporate training;
- secure development workshops;
- Security Champions programs;
- conferences and events;
- technical community meetings;
- activities that connect security and engineering.

## What remains after the game

The game creates a shared language. After the experience, terms such as risk, debt, incident, priority and consequence no longer belong exclusively to the security team.

More importantly, participants see that security is not a separate step at the end of the process. It is part of the choices that build the product during every cycle.

Codes & Consequences does not teach AppSec through memorization. It puts decisions on the table and lets the consequences do the rest.
